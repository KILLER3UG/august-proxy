### (1) Inconsistencies
- `src\sections\chat\message\AssistantMessageContent.tsx:122-130` — "N t/s" chip only when `durationMs` is present; turns restored from history without `durationMs` silently lose the chip while sibling chips (turn_end, provenance) survive restore.
- Three independent implementations of the "Reading/Read file" progress list: `tool\ToolCallItemBody.tsx:223-268`, `message\ToolCallCard.tsx:116-148`, and `ToolStepRow.tsx:36-125` (TaskItemRows) — same data, three visuals.
- `AssistantMessageContent.tsx:103-115` — ChangesCard and CircuitArtifactCard are hidden on the streaming last row, but EditRailRow/ToolStepRow diffs inside ActivitySummary render live; the same information appears then disappears at finalize.
- `MessageBubble.tsx:412-432` — comparator bails a completed row when nested Maps are unchanged, but `models` identity (fresh array per catalog refetch) and `searchQuery` highlight behave differently for tool vs tool-free rows; highlight ring lives outside the memo (`ChatThreadMessagePane.tsx:184-188`) — a completed row's `searchQuery` clear relies on that wrapper, an easy-to-miss coupling.
