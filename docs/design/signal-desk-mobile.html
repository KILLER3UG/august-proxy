How a day gets flagged

A day is flagged when its distance from the mean, in standard deviations, crosses the gate:

z = x − μ σ 1

Tuesday measures z ≈ 9.2 — far beyond the |z| > 2.5 gate.

Remember

The 2.5σ gate crosses on ~1% of ordinary days — zero or one flag per month is normal. A flag is a prompt to read the ledger, not an alarm.

μ
Mean daily tokens across the baseline window.
σ
Standard deviation — the typical distance from μ.
z
Distance from μ measured in σ; |z| > 2.5 flags a day.
